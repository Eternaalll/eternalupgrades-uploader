EternalUpgrades Uploader

A small private tool that uploads videos to the EternalUpgrades YouTube channel.

What it does

EternalUpgrades Uploader runs on the channel owner's own computer. It uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload finished videos to the EternalUpgrades channel
  • set each video's title, description, tags and thumbnail
  • schedule the publish time and add the video to a playlist

Who it is for

Only the owner of the EternalUpgrades channel. It is not a public service and nobody else can sign in to it.

Your data

The tool does not collect data from viewers or anyone else. See the privacy policy for details.
